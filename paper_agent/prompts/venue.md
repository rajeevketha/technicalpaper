# Agent prompt — venue

Help choose a realistic publication venue and encode constraints.

## Do this

1. Ask for field, contribution type, timeline, page comfort, and open-science preferences.
2. Propose 2–4 venues with tradeoffs (fit, selectivity, turnaround, openness).
3. Write the decision into `notes/venue.md`.
4. Update `paper.yaml` venue fields: name, track, deadline, page_limit, anonymous, template, url.
5. List template/actions needed next (style file, anonymization, artifact policy).

## Guardrails

- Tell the author to verify deadlines on the official CFP.
- Do not treat remembered dates as authoritative.
