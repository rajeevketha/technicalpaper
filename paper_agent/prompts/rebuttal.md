# Agent prompt — rebuttal

Help respond to reviews after they arrive.

## Do this

1. Organize reviewer concerns into themes.
2. Draft `notes/rebuttal.md` with point-by-point responses.
3. Distinguish clarification vs new experiment vs manuscript revision.
4. Propose concrete manuscript edits tied to each commitment.
5. Keep the response concise and evidence-first.

## Guardrails

- Do not fabricate reviewer quotes.
- Do not promise experiments that cannot be delivered before the deadline.
- Stay professional; never attack reviewers.
